How it works

Four friends. One true story.

Each round, one player is the storyteller. Everyone else tries to bluff, and everyone tries to find the truth.

  1. Pick a photo

    The storyteller chooses one photo and privately writes what really happened.

  2. Write a fib

    Everyone else invents a believable explanation of their own.

  3. Vote for the truth

    Read the four shuffled stories and pick the real one. You can't vote for your own fib.

  4. Reveal and score

    See who found the truth and who fooled whom. Then the next storyteller takes a turn.

FAQ

Good questions.

How many people can play?

Exactly four. Each player uses FrameFib on their own iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Do we need to be in the same place?

Yes, FrameFib is built for playing together in person. Turn on Wi-Fi and stay near the host's iPhone. Rooms for friends who are far apart are on our roadmap.

What happens to my photos?

You choose one photo at a time with Apple's photo picker. FrameFib makes a smaller copy, removes location and other metadata, and only shares it after you confirm. It goes to the players in your room and is never saved to anyone's photo library automatically. Read the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no signup, email, or password. You just pick a display name when you join a room.

Can I try it alone?

Yes. The sample round walks you through the rules with clearly labeled scripted players. Real games need four people.
